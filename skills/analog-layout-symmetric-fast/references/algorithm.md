# Fast template routing algorithm

The objective is to reduce search freedom, not checks. The default common axis is vertical x=a. Horizontal buses remain horizontal. A requested horizontal symmetry axis y=a produces top/bottom reflection without automatically rotating the PDK layer-direction policy.

## 1. Electrical inventory and local access

Map netlist → schematic device → explicit layout copy/finger → terminal → physical island → existing landing metal. Source/drain bodies may contain several isolated islands; check each. Shared-source evidence does not exempt independent drains. List B/body/guard-ring supply ties separately, even when the user mentions only G/S/D. Top-level ports require explicit boundary-access plans.

Select a legal M1 escape point for each actual MOS island. Length limits are per-run sensitivity/layout-planning values, not hardcoded µm thresholds. Follow the skill entry point's odd-metal vertical/even-metal horizontal policy: prefer vertical M1, and record controlled per-island exceptions for necessary short horizontal access without overriding hard PDK direction limits. Without M1 access, first verify PDK contacts/landings or upstream access repairs. Geometric overlap of different nets does not authorize net changes.

Before generating templates, establish legal local-access regions and cumulative M1 escape-length budgets for every device/matching group, recording regions, budgets and their basis. Derive local scope from actual contacts, nearby device/ring boundaries, escape corridors and parasitic requirements. Do not expand it arbitrarily afterward to accommodate existing long M1 paths. Here, short means the **cumulative continuous M1 path length and spatial extent** from the physical island to the first legal layer transition or local same-net collection point, not the length of one route primitive. Splitting into short segments cannot evade long-distance M1 limits. Missing budgets/boundaries permit only pending-review drafts, without invented universal µm thresholds.

## 2. Fixed topology and bounded enumeration

First reserve supply/ground capacity and channels, then route sensitive/high-impedance/differential/mirror nets, followed by ordinary connections.

Before selecting trunk coordinates, complete the full-net strategy coverage in symmetry-constraints.md: plan matched cores jointly, evaluate axis-centered or two-sided symmetric collection for common nets first, and evaluate axis-centered trunks for single-ended outputs first. Only then select side connections for explicitly noncorresponding additional branches. Side placement requires obstacle/cost evidence from preferred candidates; do not place ordinary nets on one side by default and shrink symmetry_groups to obtain PASS.

- Local: straight → two L variants → a bounded set of U variants on measured/generated tracks, without independent roaming pathfinding. L/U denotes topology rather than a single-layer polyline: assign horizontal/vertical segments to even/odd metal levels and select actual legal vias at turns. List short local-M1 exceptions separately.
- Cross-array: equal-length/corresponding M1 stubs → legal vias at corresponding positions → horizontal M2 collection → legal M2–M3 vias → vertical M3 cross-module trunks → corresponding downward transitions. Purely horizontal connections need not rise mechanically to M3. Long M1 or vertical M2 trunks cannot replace the required layered structure.
- Supply/ground: a continuous horizontal trunk with explicit extent + legal short taps, with separate supply domains. If the PDK forbids horizontal trunks on that layer, select an allowed horizontal layer and justified transitions. Do not substitute disconnected rectangles that merely appear connected. Derive trunk widths/via arrays from current/reliability requirements rather than default minimum dimensions.
- Pair nets: generate the master half-net and its exact reflected mate in one transaction, using the same layers, widths and via definitions. Keep only tracks legal on both sides. Templates explicitly connect shared trees with multiple ports; do not prune mirrored trees independently.
- Self nets: two halves plus required same-net center connections, forming one connected component for the entire net. A horizontal bus may directly cover left/right islands, but pin equidistance, mirrored taps and geometric self-symmetry still require checks.
- Two-sided collection for highly matched nets: following symmetry-constraints.md, generate one same-net trunk on each side and symmetric collection interfaces for each net. Separate nets use independent tracks and legal spacing; do not incorrectly classify them as a whole-net pair. Once adopted by the user, enumerate d_n and collection tracks jointly under this topology. Check local branch matching and whole-net connectivity separately, with independent exceptions for asymmetric additional branches.
- Matched-only arrangements such as ABBA: each net may be self-symmetric before cross-net performance matching checks, but select this objective explicitly rather than claiming mutual reflection. If parasitic budgets cannot be met, return to placement/access repair without automatically adding meanders.

Configure the track limit, stub candidates, mapped layer combinations and maximum attempts for every pair. Use `generated_tracks` to derive candidates from current pins, bounds and expanded obstacle edges; explicit tracks are optional project constraints, not built-in circuit coordinates. Exhaustion returns `NO_VALID_TEMPLATE_IN_BUDGET`, not a global-unroutability claim. Do not expand combinations indefinitely. Prioritize the shortest sensitive-net routes and minimum necessary transitions/bends before total length/area; PDK legality and connectivity always take precedence.

## 3. Joint obstacle checks on both sides

First complete the rule-source/applicability table in `drc-constraints.md`, then check its actual-metal, individual-cut, upper/lower-enclosure and cross-level-stacking contracts. Output hashed per-stage `drc-preflight.json`. Do not skip same-net cut spacing or check expanded landings only for conduction while omitting neighboring-wire spacing. FAIL/UNKNOWN for required rules affecting current geometry blocks materialization.

Use complete current PCell/ring/existing-wire/via/boundary obstacles, not just pin bboxes. Treat unknown-net obstacles as foreign. Check actual rectangle/polygon envelopes including width and spacing, all via cuts and upper/lower enclosures, stacking legality and side-of-axis boundaries. Checking only left-side reachability is insufficient. Reflecting and combining both sides' obstacles into a left-half search exclusion region is an optional simplification, but do not conflate different net ownership or special rules.

For paired connections requiring detours, use corresponding U tracks and check both sides. A one-sided obstacle does not authorize an unnecessarily long loop on the other side. Compare layer changes, alternative channels and access repair first; report tradeoffs if sensitive-node parasitics increase materially. Check incremental route-to-route shorts and via-to-foreign-metal interactions, not static obstacles alone.

## 4. Verification and environment

route_intent and svg_route_check cover connectivity and explicit reflection. They do not completely cover via envelopes, actual corner polygons, notch/EOL/min-area/PRL, high voltage, density, EM or all PCell obstacles; live/process checks are required. Strictly identical left/right templates do not exempt these checks.

Retain a direction review in run `routing-review.md`: PDK layer names→actual metal levels→required directions, H/V lengths per layer, and route ID, net, endpoints, length and disposition for every reverse-direction segment. Bind this mapping explicitly with route-spec's supported `preferred_directions`; the general engine still only warns, so zero errors does not prove direction compliance. Unapproved nonlocal reverse-direction segments fail; missing direction mappings/exception evidence are unknown. Both block materialization. Review existing PCell/ring internal shapes separately from new routes; reused old routes cannot conceal reverse-direction trunks.

At every turn, check actual via definitions, upper/lower metal connectivity, cuts/enclosures, landing space and bilateral matching. Upper/lower landings must be fully contained in actual corresponding same-net route outlines even after excluding via-supplied metal, without protruding ledges. Correct endpoint caps/extensions and widths jointly based on actual geometry, then recheck neighboring-wire spacing. M1→M3 requires legal intermediate M2 and two via levels. Same-coordinate stacking is allowed only when permitted by the process and envelope checks pass. A layer-change annotation or via symbol cannot replace actual connectivity. Recompute this evidence after candidate regeneration and saved-layout readback.

Matching assessment includes total/per-layer lengths, widths, via counts/types/stacks, branch topology, bends, contact provenance/access points, and nearest foreign nets, parallel distance/length, shields/returns/crossings and fill environment on both sides of corresponding segments. Geometric environment proxies are not coupling capacitance; confirm with final PEX/post-simulation. Reference-layout strategies are candidates rather than DRC/matching gold standards.

## 5. Routing-hierarchy acceptance table (required for candidates and readback)

Add these checks to `routing-review.md`, marking each pass/fail/unknown/not-applicable with actual route/via/island IDs and evidence, rather than textual checkmarks alone. Post-save checks must use actual readback, not copied projected metrics.

| Check | Required evidence | Failure conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Array axis and pairing | Device-centerline provenance, net/pin correspondence, reflection residuals of escape/transition points | Axis guessed from global bbox, wrong nets/missing islands, unauthorized one-sided rerouting |
| Full-net symmetry strategy | Scope inventory for all required_nets; separate reviews of matched cores, common-net collection, output axis-centered candidate comparisons and additional-branch exceptions | Input-pair-only review, default side placement of ordinary nets, unevaluated axis/two-sided alternatives, local PASS presented as overall symmetry |
| Local M1 access | Every G/S/D island, landing layers, local regions, cumulative M1 lengths/budgets, collection points or first vias | Missing islands, false poly connectivity without contacts, excessive length/outside local regions, missing budgets |
| Odd-metal vertical/even-metal horizontal directions and turns | Actual metal-level mapping, per-layer H/V lengths, per-segment reverse-direction inventory, local-M1 exceptions, legal vias/landings at every turn | Unapproved reverse-direction trunks, single-layer H/V polylines, missing transition connections, merely ignored off-preference warnings |
| DRC geometry constraints | Current deck/profile applicability, per-object width/edge spacing, same-/different-net individual-cut spacing, cross-level stacking limits, upper/lower enclosure, actual ends/corners; see drc-preflight.json | Missing rules, center distance substituted for edge clearance, skipped same-net cuts, forbidden stacks, insufficient enclosure, landing/neighbor spacing violations or stale reports |
| Cross-array/cross-block connections | Start/end macros, local M1 segments, upward via stacks, higher-layer H/V trunks and landing points for every crossing branch | Default long cross-block M1 routes, unproven transition/landing legality |
| Continuous supply/ground buses | Horizontal-trunk extent/layer/width per domain, conductor-union continuity, all taps/ring access, one connected component, IR/EM basis | Broken trunks, labels substituted for connectivity, fragmented buses connected only by serpentine branches, mixed domains |
| Left/right matching | Layer sequences/widths, per-layer lengths, via definitions/counts/positions, bends, differences in neighboring foreign nets/shields/crossings | Total-length-only comparisons, unexplained transition mismatch, material sensitive-node parasitic increases |
| Exceptions | Exact nets/islands, actual obstacle/process basis, comparison of legal alternatives, parasitic risks and user decisions when required | Broad convenience/shortness waivers for all M1 crossings or implicit asymmetry acceptance |

Buses may comprise legally touching collinear shapes; a single database path object is not required, but continuous horizontal conductors are. Not every short local net needs higher-layer routing, and existing contacts/vias must not be duplicated for superficial correspondence. For special devices offering legal access only outside M1, record PDK-constrained exceptions rather than altering PCell internals to force M1.
